Implementation Plan · nEXus UX Improvements

HAC Result Card — Zone Type & Hazard Distance Promotion

Promote Zone Type and Hazard Distance from buried flat rows to a prominent visual card at the top of Scenario and Zone HAC report panels.

📅 2026-06-01 🌿 ux-improvements 📁 6 files changed ✨ 2 new files 🔧 4 modified
Executive Summary

Zone Type and Hazard Distance are the engineering conclusion of the entire BS EN 60079-10 calculation pipeline. Currently they appear as plain label-value rows buried after intermediate outputs (dilution velocities, release characteristics, background concentrations). This plan promotes them to a visually distinct result card that sits at the top of the HAC output section — before any intermediate values — in both Scenario and Zone report panels.

The card uses a coloured header band and zone-type badge pill(s) matching the existing zone colour scheme (Zone 0 = red, Zone 1 = blue, Zone 2 = green) already used in the HAC Summary Table and 2D/3D geometry plots. Zone colours are simultaneously refactored from three hardcoded copies to a single shared constant. A new HacResultCard component is shared between both report panels. The DOCX export pipeline is unaffected.
